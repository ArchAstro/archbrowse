# Code of conduct

Be respectful, considerate, and constructive. Focus disagreement on ideas and
behavior rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
sexualized attention, intimidation, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to **security@archastro.ai**. Maintainers will review
reports, protect reporters' privacy where possible, and may remove content or
restrict participation. Maintainers involved in a report will not participate
in its resolution.

This policy applies to the repository, issues, reviews, and other official
ArchBrowse community spaces.
